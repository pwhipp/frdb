About FRDB

FRDB is an open access forensic research summary database focused on published evidence recovery comparison research. It brings key findings into a searchable resource for researchers, practitioners, and students who need to compare recovery methods, tested equipment, biological materials, substrates, and related study details.

The database is designed to support evidence-based practice and further academic inquiry by making relevant forensic research easier to find, filter, and review. Its current focus is evidence recovery, with scope to grow into other forensic research areas as the resource develops.

Community contributions help keep FRDB useful and current. Suggested additions and reported issues are reviewed before publication so that the public data remains reliable, moderated, and suitable for forensic science use.
